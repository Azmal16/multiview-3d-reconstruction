Motivation & Problem Statement

MM 805 · Final Project

The Core Challenge

Reconstructing 3D geometry from sparse 2D observations is inherently ill-posed — depth ambiguity and occlusion make it impossible to recover a unique solution from limited views.

Why It Matters

  • Autonomous vehicles & robotics need fast 3D scene understanding
  • AR/VR applications require real-time object reconstruction
  • Medical imaging reconstructs 3D anatomy from 2D scans
  • E-commerce needs 3D product previews from product photos

Research Question

How does reconstruction quality evolve as the number of input views increases from 1 → 5, and which method best handles limited observations?

Methods & Architectures

MM 805 · Final Project
3D-R2N2
Choy et al., ECCV 2016  ·  17.5M parameters
Recurrent 3D-GRU that sequentially updates a voxel grid as each new view arrives. Encoder compresses 128×128 images to 1024-d features; 3D transposed convolutions decode to 32³ occupancy.
Pix2Vox
Xie et al., ICCV 2019  ·  12.7M parameters
Context-aware fusion: each view produces an independent coarse volume; a scoring network learns per-voxel weights to adaptively fuse views. A 3D residual refiner sharpens the result.
TripoSR (New Baseline)
Tochilkin et al., 2024  ·  ~1GB weights  ·  Zero-shot
Feed-forward transformer trained on Objaverse. Single image → triplane NeRF in one forward pass. Density field queried at grid points for voxel evaluation; mesh extracted via marching cubes.

Training Setup (R2N2 & Pix2Vox)

Loss: Binary Cross-Entropy on voxel occupancy  |  Optimizer: Adam (lr=1e-4)  |  Epochs: 20  |  Best checkpoint saved & reused

TripoSR — No Training Needed

Pre-trained on Objaverse (800K+ objects). Inference: ~0.5–1s/image on GPU. Background removal via rembg, composited on gray (0.5) background as per official pipeline.

Key Difference

R2N2 & Pix2Vox are trained on this dataset.
TripoSR is zero-shot — never sees our data.

Why TripoSR Scores Lower on Synthetic Data

  • Trained on Objaverse (real photos) — synthetic silhouettes are out-of-distribution
  • Orientation mismatch — no canonical alignment with GT voxels
  • Optimized for mesh visual quality, not voxel IoU

Where TripoSR Wins

  • Real photographs — trained on realistic data
  • Produces high-quality meshes (.obj), not just voxels
  • No training required — works on any object category
  • Handles in-the-wild images via background removal

Adaptive Selection (R2N2 + Pix2Vox)

Routes each (category, views) pair to the best model. Always selects Pix2Vox — confirming its uniform dominance on synthetic data.

Conclusions & Future Work

MM 805 · Final Project

Key Findings

1
More views = better quality — but diminishing returns appear beyond 3–4 views in all categories.
2
Pix2Vox dominates uniformly — context-aware fusion outperforms recurrent GRU across every (category, view) pair.
3
Category difficulty varies significantly — cars reconstruct best; chairs (thin legs, high occlusion) are hardest for all methods.
4
TripoSR excels on real images — zero-shot performance on in-the-wild photos surpasses trained voxel models in visual quality.
5
Voxel resolution is the bottleneck — 32³ limits geometric detail; TripoSR's mesh output is inherently higher fidelity.

Limitations

  • 32³ voxel resolution caps geometric fidelity for all methods
  • Synthetic silhouettes are simpler than real ShapeNet renders
  • TripoSR IoU comparison is domain-mismatched (unfair but informative)
  • Training on 3 categories only — limited generalization scope

Future Work

  • Benchmark 3D Gaussian Splatting (SplatterImage, pixelSplat) under identical few-view conditions
  • Optimal viewpoint selection — which N angles maximize reconstruction quality?
  • Web platform — upload a photo, get a 3D mesh (inference.py backend ready)
  • Evaluate on real ShapeNet data for fair cross-method comparison
